How to Play

The DEUjo offers exercises - called katas - for different grammar areas (nouns, verbs, ...). Pick a kata on the landing page and start training.

Scoring

Your answers are marked in place. ✅ green is correct, ❌ red is wrong, and a wrong field shows the answer you needed next to it.

Every correct answer adds to your streak — the 🔥 counter up top counts consecutive hits across all katas, and any wrong answer resets it. It also earns belt progress for the kata you are training: ⚪ 🟡 🟠 🟢 🔵 🟤 ⚫.

A correct answer is worth 1 belt point, a wrong one costs 1, and skipping costs 0.5. Every 5 points promote you to the next belt. Belts are tracked per kata; the streak is not. Progress is saved locally.
